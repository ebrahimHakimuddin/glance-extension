# Privacy Policy: Glance Chrome

Glance Chrome does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data, and has no servers or analytics.

- Your configuration (`glance.yml`, secrets, includes) is stored locally in `chrome.storage` and never leaves your browser except as part of requests you configure.
- Widgets contact only the URLs and services you configure (e.g. RSS feeds, APIs, Reddit, Docker Hub, weather providers), directly from your browser.
- Icon images may be loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net when you use `si:`, `di:`, `mdi:` or `sh:` icons.
